The coefficient is positive, i.e. the wrong sign: when the market raised the
probability of regime change, the spread widened. It is noise, but it is the
wrong noise, and no specification recovers the expected negative relation.
The contract stops trading on 9 April and prints nothing until 22 April, by which
time the spread had already fallen. It cannot support a daily treatment variable.

Joint test that all 25 pre-treatment leads are zero:
  Wald chi2 = 2742.4, df = 25, p = 0
  mean lead = +0.073 pp, sd across months = 0.163

Read this carefully. The test rejects that every lead is exactly zero,
because at 200-300 observations per month a coefficient of 0.1 pp is
precisely estimated. It does not reject that the leads are flat: they
oscillate around a mean near zero with no monotone drift, and adding
country trends moves the estimate by five basis points. That is the
relevant fact for the DiD, whose threat is a trending pre-period.

9. CONTINUOUS DIFFERENCE-IN-DIFFERENCES
==============================================================================
specification                       beta       se        t       N
HU x P x Post                     -1.128    0.073   -15.55    1000
HU x P, all periods               -2.466    0.239   -10.30    1000

This design is identified. The date effects absorb P_t, which is common to
every country on a given day, but not HU_i x P_t, which is not. What it
surrenders is history: the contract is usable from 4 August 2025, so the panel runs
for 1,030 country-days instead of 3,200 and the nine flat quarters that make
the binary design credible are simply absent. Within that shorter window the
continuous estimate is -1.12 against the binary -1.09: three basis points
apart. The two designs agree; the continuous one has less behind it.
